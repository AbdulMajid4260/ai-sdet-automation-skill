# Flow 2 — Dynamic Cheapest Product

## Target

https://www.saucedemo.com/

## Test steps

1. Open the SauceDemo website.
2. Log in using the standard demo username `standard_user` and password `secret_sauce`.
3. Verify that the products page is displayed.
4. Sort the products by price from low to high.
5. Determine the cheapest product from the displayed product data rather than hardcoding a product name.
6. Add the cheapest product to the cart.
7. Open the shopping cart.
8. Verify that the cart contains exactly one item.
9. Verify that the product in the cart is the product that was identified as the cheapest.
