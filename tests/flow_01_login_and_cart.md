# Flow 1 — Login and Add Product to Cart

## Target

https://www.saucedemo.com/

## Test steps

1. Open the SauceDemo website.
2. Log in using the standard demo username `standard_user` and password `secret_sauce`.
3. Verify that the products page is displayed after successful login.
4. Identify the product `Sauce Labs Backpack`.
5. Add `Sauce Labs Backpack` to the shopping cart.
6. Open the shopping cart.
7. Verify that `Sauce Labs Backpack` is present in the cart.
8. Verify that the cart contains exactly one item.
9. Verify that the selected product is the expected product.
